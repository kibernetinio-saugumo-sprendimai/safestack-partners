# Code of Conduct

SafeStack welcomes respectful, constructive participation from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Participants must:

- communicate with respect and assume good faith;
- focus criticism on ideas, text and technical evidence;
- protect private information and avoid publishing secrets;
- respect project, platform and authorized-testing boundaries.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, malicious disclosure and instructions to access systems without permission are not acceptable.

Report a concern privately through the repository's security reporting path or the SafeStack organization profile. Maintainers may edit, hide or close content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors and the project.
